Case Study

The billing page nobody had data for

A new account billing page needed to ship — a fresh design, no precedent to copy. There was no sample data and no test data to build against. Even the test team didn't have scenarios to validate it with.

01 · The gap
No sample or test data existed for a fully new feature — nothing to build or validate against.
02 · The build
Worked directly from the design team's variants and schema to build the UI end-to-end, on schedule.
03 · The safeguard
Shipped behind a feature flag, then validated live in production against the real scenarios the test team did have.
04 · The result
Two months post-launch, a customer wrote in — unprompted — about the new page.
"Hey, love the new format of our account on the line — awesome, easy reading, and well done." — End customer, via client support channel

The client's account team noted that direct positive customer feedback like this is rare — most quality signals in enterprise software are indirect.
